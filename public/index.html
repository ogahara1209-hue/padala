How to deposit

  1. Buy USDC with yen at a registered exchange (SBI VC Trade)
  2. Register the address above as the withdrawal destination (recipient: yourself; destination: "Other" → "Padala"; country: your country)
  3. Withdraw. Requests before 10:00 on weekdays usually arrive by the next banking day

Padala never receives yen and never buys USDC. The exchange sells it; you hold it.

USDC is received on the Ethereum network only. The network fee (gas) for sending is paid by Padala out of the service fee.
